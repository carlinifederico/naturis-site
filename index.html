"Technology is nature too..."

A live immersive experience where code and ecosystem merge into a single pulse. NATURIS invites us to reconnect the human, the digital, and the organic in one continuous flow of balance and evolution.

by Jorge Tereso, Federico Carlini and Fernando Maldonado

Room 1

"All is connected"

Immersive Experience

Real-time tracking turns each person into a glowing mycelial reflection built from colored fungal threads, scaled by their distance to each surface. As people move closer, luminous filaments link their silhouettes, revealing how fungal networks communicate.

Concept:humans as living nodes in nature's wood-wide web — connection as infrastructure.

Room 2

"Let go and fall"

Immersive Experience

An upside-down city hangs above the forest, and wherever you stand, its objects rain down into the green. Your presence sets the fall in motion — a reminder that we are never outside the systems we disturb.

Room 3

"Evolution"

Immersive Experience

Visitors place small cubes on the floor and projected light flows out from each cube, weaving across the room and rising up mirrored columns into infinity.

Concept:Inside the living mind of nature, every small understanding we spark becomes evolution.

Room 4

"Traces of the footprints"

Immersive Experience

As spectators move, the surface reacts: human parcels grow across the land — agricultural plots, roads and constructions that spread the more people move. Each gesture expands the human footprint over nature.

Concept:the mark of man over the living world, and the search for balance.

Room 5

"The shape of music"

Immersive Experience

Creatures that pass unnoticed every day are magnified until you can finally see their genius. From day to night, the forest keeps running its oldest, most refined technology: life itself.

Room 6

"Footprints of memory"

Immersive Experience

Visitors drift across a floor of fine white stones, and every dragging step makes ecosystems of translucent petals bloom and dissolve on towering rocks.

Concept:Whatever we step on, nature moves forward.

"Out of this storm comes rhythm, balance, and a strange sense of harmony."
